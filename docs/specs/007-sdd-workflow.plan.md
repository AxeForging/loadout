## Risks

- **Parser correctness is the whole feature** — a mutation that clobbers a byte outside the checkbox violates the spec's byte-preservation rule. Mitigated by the exhaustive table tests (fences, CRLF, nesting, idempotency) written **first** (TDD per team convention).
- **`gh` output shape drift**: pin to `--json` fields and URL parsing with tolerant regexes; stub-based tests lock our side of the contract, real-`gh` breakage surfaces as the actionable error path, not corruption.
- **`sdd.yaml` merge conflicts on `Issues:` map** in busy repos — values are per-spec keys, so conflicts are line-local and trivially resolvable; documented in README.
- Rollback: plain revert — step-ID comments and `sdd.yaml` left in repos are inert markdown/YAML.
